About JAKH

A friendly bilingual place to think, learn, and play

JAKH is a free English-and-Arabic riddle, quiz, and browser-game website. The library currently includes 3,553 question cards mapped into 56 topics and five clear sections.

JAKH موقع مجاني للألغاز والاختبارات والألعاب باللغتين العربية والإنجليزية. تضم المكتبة حالياً 3,553 بطاقة سؤال موزعة على 56 موضوعاً وخمسة أقسام واضحة.

How content is organized

Every question belongs to one category, one practical subtopic, and one difficulty level. Related categories and focused collections help people move through the library without a maze of overlapping pages.

Review and translation

Questions require complete English and Arabic prompts and answers. Automated audits catch missing fields, taxonomy drift, and exact duplicates; high-traffic collections receive an additional meaning and answer-parity review before publication.

Accuracy and corrections

Evergreen facts are preferred. Time-sensitive facts and professional topics need extra review. You can flag an individual card from its share/report controls or send a correction through the suggestion box in the Mind Lab.

Educational boundaries

JAKH is for learning and entertainment. Medical, legal, financial, pharmacy, and psychology questions are not professional advice and should not be used to make personal decisions.

Independence

Fan-made quizzes are clearly labelled and do not imply affiliation, endorsement, or ownership of third-party names or marks. JAKH does not use unlicensed character artwork in its focused collections.

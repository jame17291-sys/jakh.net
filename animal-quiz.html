Animal quiz questions with answers

This animal quiz begins with the favourites, the cheetah, the blue whale, the chameleon and the ship of the desert, then goes to the animals of this region: the fennec fox of the Sahara, the Arabian oryx that came back from extinction in the wild, the dugongs of the Gulf and the Arabian leopard of Dhofar. The hard questions ask why a giraffe needs such high blood pressure and what decides a sea turtle's sex, and the last five are for the naturalists: oval camel blood cells, sandgrouse that carry water in their feathers and the swift that stays airborne for ten months. Every answer is explained in English and Arabic.

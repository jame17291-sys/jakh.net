01Which ancient civilization built the pyramids at Giza?

Answer

The ancient Egyptians during the Old Kingdom.

02What ancient civilization of Mesopotamia is credited with creating the world's earliest known writing system?

Answer

The Sumerians, who developed cuneiform writing around 3200 BC.

Full topic: Middle East History
03What ancient empire was based in Persia and at its peak stretched from Egypt to India?

Answer

The Achaemenid (Persian) Empire, founded by Cyrus the Great in 550 BC.

Full topic: Middle East History
04In which city was Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) born?

Answer

Mecca (Makkah), in the Arabian Peninsula, around 570 CE.

Full topic: Middle East History
05What year did the Prophet Muhammad migrate from Mecca to Medina, marking the start of the Islamic calendar?

Answer

622 CE — the Hijra. The Islamic calendar (AH) is dated from this event.

Full topic: Middle East History
06Which dynasty made Damascus its capital and ruled the early Islamic caliphate from 661 to 750 CE?

Answer

The Umayyad dynasty.

Full topic: Middle East History
07Into which language were many Greek scientific and philosophical works translated during the Abbasid translation movement?

Answer

Arabic.

Full topic: Middle East History
08Who was Saladin and what is he best known for in history?

Answer

Saladin (Salah al-Din al-Ayyubi) was the Muslim Kurdish leader who founded the Ayyubid dynasty and is most famous for recapturing Jerusalem from the Crusaders in 1187.

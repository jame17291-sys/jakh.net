01Which ancient civilization built the pyramids at Giza?

Answer

The ancient Egyptians, during the Old Kingdom period (c. 2580–2560 BC).

07What was the Bayt al-Hikma (House of Wisdom) in Baghdad?

Answer

A major intellectual and scholarly center in Abbasid Baghdad, where scholars translated Greek, Persian, and Indian texts into Arabic and produced advances in mathematics, medicine, astronomy, and philosophy during the Islamic Golden Age.

08Who was Saladin and what is he best known for in history?

Answer

Saladin (Salah al-Din al-Ayyubi) was the Muslim Kurdish leader who founded the Ayyubid dynasty and is most famous for recapturing Jerusalem from the Crusaders in 1187.

81What is collision theory in chemical kinetics?

Answer

Reactions occur when reactant molecules collide with sufficient energy (≥ activation energy) and correct orientation

Answer

Guidelines for predicting the feasibility of ring-closure reactions based on ring size and bond type at the closure site

83What is the Born-Oppenheimer approximation?

Answer

Nuclear motion and electronic motion can be treated separately because nuclei are much heavier and move slower than electrons

84What is a Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution?

Answer

A statistical distribution describing the range of speeds of molecules in a gas at a given temperature

Answer

A cycloaddition between a conjugated diene and a dienophile to form a six-membered ring

86What is hyperconjugation?

Answer

The stabilising interaction between σ-bonding electrons and an adjacent empty or partially filled p-orbital or π-system

87What is the Hammett equation used for?

Answer

To predict the effect of substituents on the rate and equilibrium of organic reactions at an aromatic ring

88What is the distinction between kinetic and thermodynamic control in reactions?

Answer

Kinetic control favours the fastest-forming product; thermodynamic control favours the most stable product

89What is the Franck-Condon principle?

Answer

Electronic transitions occur so quickly that nuclei do not move during the transition — the most probable transition preserves nuclear geometry

90What is resonance in organic chemistry?

Answer

A way of representing molecules that cannot be accurately described by a single Lewis structure — the actual structure is a hybrid of resonance contributors

A reaction coupling an alcohol with an acidic pronucleophile (e.g. carboxylic acid) with inversion of stereochemistry

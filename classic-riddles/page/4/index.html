61A man is found dead in a locked room. The only items in the room are a pool of water, a window, and a rope. How did he die?

Answer

He stood on a block of ice with the rope around his neck; the ice melted, leaving only water

76A man lives on floor 30 and rides down each morning. Returning alone, he rides to floor 15 and walks up; on rainy days or with another rider, he reaches floor 30. Why?

Answer

He is short and can reach only the floor-15 button. His umbrella reaches the floor-30 button on rainy days; otherwise, another rider can press it for him.

77You have weights of 1, 3, 9, and 27 units and a two-pan balance. Weights may be placed on either pan. How can you balance an object weighing 20 units?

Answer

Put the object with the 9- and 1-unit weights on one pan, and the 27- and 3-unit weights on the other: 20+9+1 = 27+3

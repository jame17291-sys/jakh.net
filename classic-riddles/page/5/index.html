85An 8-liter container is full, while unmarked 5-liter and 3-liter containers are empty. By pouring only until a container is full or empty, how can you split the water into two equal 4-liter portions?

Answer

Track amounts as (8L,5L,3L): (8,0,0) → (3,5,0) → (3,2,3) → (6,2,0) → (6,0,2) → (1,5,2) → (1,4,3) → (4,4,0)

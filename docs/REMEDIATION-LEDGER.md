## Executive reconciliation

The audit contains **81 detailed finding IDs**: 80 were open at the audit cutoff and `I18N-01` was already resolved. Its `H-01`–`H-17` register is a summary alias layer over those detailed findings, not 17 additional defects. The current implementation classification is:

| Status | Count | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Remediated | 60 | 59 have a concrete remediation in this branch; `I18N-01` was already resolved in the audit baseline. None of the branch work is live until reviewed, merged, and deployed. |
| Mitigated / pending activation | 13 | The branch materially reduces the risk, but the original concern is only partly closed, depends on protected production activation, or still needs owner/external work. |
| Residual | 8 | The original concern remains materially open. The principal causes are external infrastructure, legal/editorial ownership, static-host limitations, or deferred architecture. |
| **Total** | **81** | Complete one-to-one mapping of audit finding IDs. |

This is not a claim that the website is “100% fact-checked” or fully certified across browsers and assistive technology. The implementation establishes a detailed, testable remediation baseline and makes remaining work explicit. In particular, only **4 of 3,553 cards are source-reviewed**; **3,549 remain pending**, including **all 278 high-priority safety-sensitive cards**. There are also **94 heuristic mutable-language candidates** awaiting human review.

## Explicit residual and external-action register

The following items must remain open in any release note until independently completed:

1. **No production deployment:** every status above is branch-only. Live behavior remains the audit baseline until activation succeeds.
2. **Off-account encrypted backup:** Time Travel evidence and a same-provider restore drill do not prove an encrypted export stored under a separate account/trust boundary.
3. **Protected environments, reviewers, and secrets:** repository references to `production` and `recovery-drill` do not prove required reviewers, branch protection, or least-privilege secrets are configured. Required secrets include the Cloudflare deployment token/account ID, recovery-read token, drill-only token/database ID, and repository issue permission.
4. **Static Pages rollback:** monitoring a bad Pages release does not restore the prior artifact.
5. **Static response headers, compression, and caching:** CSP/HSTS/framing/nosniff/referrer/permissions headers, Brotli, and immutable asset policy require an edge or different host.
6. **DNS and mail:** DNSSEC/DS, CAA, SPF/DMARC alignment/enforcement, DKIM placement, MTA-STS/TLS-RPT, optional BIMI/IPv6, and preload policy are external-account decisions.
7. **Legal facts:** legal entity/controller, postal/privacy contact, governing law, complaint/transfer text, Terms, and minors/parental-consent policy require truthful owner/legal input.
8. **HTTP 301 aliases:** `.html` and redundant `/index.html` aliases need edge redirects; HTML canonical tags are not redirects.
9. **Frontend architecture:** `app.js` and shared CSS remain route-agnostic; code/CSS splitting and search-index architecture are deferred.
10. **Editorial completion:** 3,549 cards remain pending; 278/278 high-stakes cards are pending; 94 mutable-language candidates need human disposition. Four reviewed cards do not certify the corpus.
11. **Physical/browser/assistive testing:** Safari, Firefox, physical iOS/Android, screen readers, forced colors, formal zoom, touch accuracy, and full game/admin playthroughs remain unverified.

## Ordered activation checklist

The sequence below is deliberately fail-closed. Stop at the first unmet gate and retain the last known-good state.

1. **Freeze and identify the candidate.** Record the exact merge commit, generated-file state, Worker version, expected schema 8, migrations 0007–0008, and all artifact checksums. Confirm the worktree is clean and reviewed.
2. **Close governance blockers before mutation.** Configure required branch checks, `production` and `recovery-drill` environments, required reviewers, and least-privilege secrets. Confirm repository issue alerts can be created. Do not infer these settings from workflow YAML.
3. **Establish recoverability.** Capture current Worker/D1 state and a Time Travel bookmark. Create an encrypted, checksummed, **off-account** D1 export; restore it into an isolated non-production database and retain the result. If this cannot be proved, do not run `migrate-final`.
4. **Run the exact-commit validation matrix.** Require V-REL, V-STATIC, V-SEO, V-CONTRACT, V-IMG, V-GAME, V-WORKER, and V-BROWSER to pass with zero skipped required tests. Review any changed baseline rather than lowering thresholds.
5. **Merge through protected `main`.** Let Pages publish only after all required checks. Verify canonical count, sitemap, physical Arabic routes, security.txt, offline fallback, responsive images, and no broken internal resource. Remember that current Pages hosting cannot auto-rollback.
6. **Run API `compatibility`.** This phase must deploy the exact schema-tolerant Worker without applying a D1 migration or changing the recorded bookmark. Require health and monitor success plus receipt proof that D1 is unchanged.
7. **Observe compatibility.** Confirm public health, schema compatibility range, anonymous session 200 behavior, CORS, monitor issue routing, and frontend behavior. Roll back to the receipt’s exact prior Worker on failure.
8. **Run API `migrate-final`.** Only the exact already-active compatibility Worker from the candidate commit may authorize migrations 0007–0008. Capture the pre-migration recovery evidence, apply migrations once, prove that the same Worker remains healthy on schema 8, then deploy/verify the final Worker.
9. **Verify accounts with a dedicated non-production/synthetic identity.** Test registration and one-time recovery receipt, logout, password-session rotation, recovery reset/rotation, consent allow/deny, privacy request, export/audit redaction, server-checked challenge/cancel/submit, and permanent deletion. Never use a real user account for destructive smoke tests.
10. **Verify content and discovery.** Crawl all 424 sitemap URLs; assert every one of 3,553 card IDs occurs exactly once per language across crawlable pagination; check 16 reciprocal shared-language pairs and raster social metadata. Submit the sitemap and monitor index coverage.
11. **Activate external controls separately.** Apply tested edge headers/caching/compression and exact alias 301s; then DNS/mail changes in a staged, monitored rollout. Each change needs its own rollback path and live verification.
12. **Run post-release observation.** Monitor immediately, after cache propagation, and through at least one scheduled cleanup window. Retain release, monitor, backup, restore, coverage, integration, browser, and crawl evidence together.
13. **Continue editorial and accessibility closure.** Prioritize all 278 high-stakes cards, then 94 mutable candidates, then the remainder. Run the physical/browser/assistive matrix before claiming the corresponding support or conformance level.

## Final acceptance criteria

### Minimum acceptance for deploying this remediation

- The exact release commit is on protected `main`, reviewed, and clean.
- All repeatable evidence keys V-REL through V-BROWSER are green for that commit; Worker thresholds remain at least 80% lines, 70% branches, and 85% functions.
- Generated state is deterministic: 427 HTML files, 424 canonical/sitemap URLs, 56 categories, 3,553 cards, 112 localized topic pages, and 16 physical shared Arabic routes.
- A current encrypted off-account backup has a verified checksum and successful isolated restore, in addition to the Time Travel bookmark.
- Required environment reviewers and least-privilege secrets are independently confirmed.
- The compatibility deployment proves no D1 mutation; the final phase proves exact-commit compatibility before applying migrations 0007–0008 and ends healthy on schema 8.
- Production monitoring and its incident issue route are exercised; rollback targets and receipts are retained.
- Pages and API smoke tests pass without weakening truthfulness: scores are server-checked/not proctored, fact review remains separate, offline scope remains bounded, and account/recovery limitations remain disclosed.
- No unresolved failure is hidden by retrying, suppressing, lowering thresholds, or editing generated output by hand.

### Acceptance required to call the original audit fully closed

The branch alone cannot satisfy this stronger standard. Full closure additionally requires:

- an exercised static rollback mechanism;
- verified production static security headers, Brotli/cache policy, and edge 301 aliases;
- approved DNS/mail hardening;
- truthful owner/legal entity, governing-law, Terms, transfer/complaint, and minors policy;
- source-backed human review of all 3,553 cards, with all 278 high-stakes cards completed before any safety-sensitive verification claim;
- disposition and scheduled revalidation of all 94 mutable-language candidates;
- a measured `app.js`/CSS/search architecture split meeting agreed performance budgets;
- supported-browser, physical-device, screen-reader, forced-colors, zoom, touch, game, and authenticated-admin acceptance evidence;
- production proof that the deployed revisions and settings match this ledger.

Until those criteria are met, release language should say **“the implementation remediation plan is complete in the isolated branch, with documented residual and activation work”**, not “all audit findings are fixed” or “the website is 100% verified.”

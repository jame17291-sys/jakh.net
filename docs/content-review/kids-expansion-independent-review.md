# Kids expansion: independent editorial review

Reviewed 30 September 2026. This expansion adds 360 bilingual question-based activities to the original 120: every learning area now has 80 entries, with 20 in each age band. The 120 original activities remain byte-for-byte unchanged. The 30 legacy riddles retain their own collection and identifiers.

## Review performed

The coordinating reviewer read every new English and Arabic core question, answer and explanation independently of the three authoring agents. The authors checked the full schema, metadata, instructions, easier/harder variations and age/area coverage. A second reviewer separately read all 120 creativity/life objectives, materials and variations, including every final `-20` entry. Numerical and constrained logic checks are recorded in the authors' notes.

Corrections made before freezing the sources included:

- Removed a conservation-of-number puzzle overlapping a maths activity; replaced it with a hidden-ribbon inference.
- Replaced an older language survey question overlapping the existing evidence-editing activity with interpreting the uncertainty in “likely”.
- Clarified chronological ordering, scale-pan comparisons, shape corners, and parent-friendly sound prompts.
- Adapted Arabic ambiguity and early-language questions to natural Arabic instead of literal English wordplay. Removed unnecessary implementation commentary from child-facing explanations.
- Replaced ambiguous Arabic coordinate pairs, negative-temperature notation, letter swaps and Sudoku rows with explicit words or positions. Isolated arithmetic and fractions for RTL display in HTML and PDF output.
- Corrected scientific wording about heat transfer, winged seeds and ordinary paper filtration, and checked qualified explanations against the primary references in the science authoring note.
- Replaced broad repeated learning objectives with specific purposes, aligned two life-skills titles/objectives with their actual questions, clarified drawing/preparation needs, and made open-ended answers examples rather than mandatory responses.
- Kept older creative and cooperative prompts concrete and removed unnecessary workplace terminology.

Each new item has its own bilingual title, standalone question, objective, hint, explained answer, variations and extension. Repeated scaffolding gives a consistent parent routine; it is not counted as separate content. Exact normalized title/prompt and ID checks cover all 480 entries in both languages. They complement the editorial review and do not by themselves establish semantic uniqueness.

All 24 packs now reference 20 activities each. The 16 existing weekly plans retain five distinct activities each; eight parent guides and original progress compatibility are preserved.

## Limits and release evidence

This is AI editorial review, not qualified educator approval, a native-speaker certification or a report of physical trials with families. Age pathways remain adjustable enrichment guidance, not developmental assessments or curriculum claims. The exact reviewed sources are bound by SHA-256 in `kids-release-review.json`. Rendered PDF and preview checks are recorded separately in the print and browser review notes.

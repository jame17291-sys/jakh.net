# Older kids activity editorial review

Review date: 30 September 2026.

## Reviewed material

`data/kids/activities-older.json` contains 60 distinct activities: 30 for ages 7–8 and 30 for ages 9–12. Each age has five activities in each of language, maths, logic, science, creativity and everyday skills. Every activity has English and Modern Standard Arabic content, four actionable steps, an objective, materials and substitutes, preparation, discussion question, hint, answer or response guidance, explanation, easier and harder options, extension and supervision guidance. All are screen-free and require no printer or external assets.

This is an AI editorial and programmatic review, not an independent educator, Arabic-language professional, accessibility specialist or child-development assessment. No classroom or family user testing has been performed. Age bands are suggested starting points and activities include adaptations; no curriculum alignment or clinical outcomes are claimed.

## Editorial checks completed

- Checked each core task for sufficient givens, feasible household materials, a concrete example, usable steps and a corresponding answer or open-ended success criterion.
- Checked English/Arabic instructional equivalence. The acrostic activity intentionally uses **SUN** in English and **حلم** in Arabic, preserving the three-line initial-letter task with natural language-specific content. Arabic code and route descriptions explicitly define left-to-right order where necessary.
- Reviewed age differentiation: ages 7–8 use concrete representation, short texts and direct matching; ages 9–12 introduce evidence limits, multiple constraints, ratios, experimental controls, revision and tradeoffs.
- Reviewed experiments for honest uncertainty: paper strength, absorption, shadows, falling paper, dissolving, insulation, vibration, friction and day/night modelling distinguish expected tendencies from guaranteed observations. No results are represented as already observed.
- Reviewed practical supervision. Water, ice, lights, reused packaging, rulers and ramps have task-specific precautions. Activities avoid hot water, flames, blades used by children, real river crossings, real passwords, publishing children's details, purchases and exposure to strangers.
- Reviewed open-ended work for multiple acceptable responses and respectful participation. Pattern predictions and ambiguous text explicitly avoid asserting unsupported unique answers.
- Checked duration buckets against the agreed schema. Two 25-minute drafts were assigned the supported 30-minute bucket. The ice session explicitly includes a twenty-minute observation interval within its thirty-minute duration.
- Corrected probability-draw metadata to household materials because its setup requires a covered container. Clarified the ruler's required condition. Added both tied solutions when the network's C–D link is removed.

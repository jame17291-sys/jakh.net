What is the name of the outermost solid layer of the Earth?

The crust.

🪨 Geology Easy Plate Tectonics & Earth Structure

What is the name of the thick layer between the crust and the core?

The mantle.

🪨 Geology Easy Plate Tectonics & Earth Structure

What is the term for molten rock that is still beneath the Earth's surface?

Magma.

🪨 Geology Easy Volcanoes & Volcanology

What do we call magma after it has erupted onto the surface?

Lava.

🪨 Geology Easy Rocks, Petrology & Rock Cycle

What type of rock is formed when magma or lava cools and solidifies?

Igneous rock.

🪨 Geology Easy Rocks, Petrology & Rock Cycle

What type of rock forms from layers of compressed sediment over time?

Sedimentary rock.

🪨 Geology Easy Rocks, Petrology & Rock Cycle

What is the name for rocks that have been changed by intense heat and pressure?

Metamorphic rock.

What is the hardest natural mineral on the Mohs scale?

Diamond, with a hardness of 10.

🪨 Geology Easy Earthquakes & Seismology

What is the term for the point on the Earth's surface directly above where an earthquake originates?

The epicenter.

🪨 Geology Easy Surface Processes & Landforms

What is the process called when rocks and soil are worn away and carried elsewhere by water, wind, or ice?

Erosion.

🪨 Geology Easy Plate Tectonics & Earth Structure

What are the large pieces of the Earth's crust that slowly move called?

Tectonic plates.

🪨 Geology Easy Minerals & Geochemistry

What very common mineral is made of silicon and oxygen and is found in sand?

Quartz.

🪨 Geology Easy Surface Processes & Landforms

What is the term for a crack in the Earth's crust where two blocks of rock have moved past each other?

A fault.

🪨 Geology Easy Geological Time & Fossils

What is the preserved remains or trace of an ancient organism found in rock called?

A fossil.

🪨 Geology Easy Rocks, Petrology & Rock Cycle

What dense, dark volcanic rock covers much of the ocean floor?

Basalt.

🪨 Geology Medium Plate Tectonics & Earth Structure

What is the process called when one tectonic plate slides beneath another into the mantle?

Subduction.

🪨 Geology Medium Rocks, Petrology & Rock Cycle

What coarse-grained igneous rock, often pink or grey, is widely used in construction?

Granite.

The Richter scale.

Pangaea.

31What is the difference between a shield volcano and a stratovolcano?

Answer

A shield volcano has gentle slopes and erupts low-viscosity lava over large areas. A stratovolcano (composite volcano) has steep sides and erupts thick lava, ash, and pyroclastic material explosively.

39What is isostasy in geology?

Answer

The state of gravitational equilibrium between the Earth's crust (lithosphere) and the underlying mantle. Lighter crust 'floats' higher on the denser mantle, similar to how ice floats on water.

40What is the difference between the Richter scale and the Moment Magnitude Scale (Mw)?

Answer

The Richter scale measures seismograph amplitude and was calibrated for local earthquakes. The Moment Magnitude Scale (Mw) measures the total energy released and is more accurate for large or distant earthquakes. Modern seismologists use Mw almost exclusively.

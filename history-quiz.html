Across the centuries

History quiz questions with answers

This history quiz starts with dates most people know, the Titanic, the first steps on the Moon and the fall of the Berlin Wall, then widens to the whole world: Tariq ibn Ziyad crossing into Spain, Suleiman the Magnificent, Mehmed II at Constantinople and the Umayyad capital in al-Andalus. The hard questions move to Mali, Mughal India, the Aztecs and Samarkand, and the last five are for the historians: Zheng He's treasure fleets, Menelik II at Adwa, Korea's turtle ships and the volcano that cancelled a summer. Every answer is explained in English and Arabic.

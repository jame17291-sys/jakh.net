🚦 Infrastructure Systems Medium Bridges & Structural Engineering

What type of bridge uses diagonal cables running from towers directly to the deck, without a main horizontal cable?

A cable-stayed bridge. Unlike suspension bridges, the cables connect directly to the deck.

🚦 Infrastructure Systems Medium Water & Wastewater Systems

What is the purpose of a water treatment plant?

To remove contaminants and pathogens from raw water sources to make it safe for drinking and domestic use.

🚦 Infrastructure Systems Medium Power & Energy Systems

What does load shedding mean in an electrical power grid?

Temporarily reducing or disconnecting demand to keep supply and demand balanced and prevent grid collapse.

🚦 Infrastructure Systems Medium Ports, Canals & Airports

What is a lock in canal engineering?

A section of canal enclosed by gates that allows boats to be raised or lowered between water levels of different elevations.

🚦 Infrastructure Systems Medium Railways & Tunnels

What machine uses rotating cutting heads to bore tunnels through rock or soil?

A Tunnel Boring Machine (TBM).

🚦 Infrastructure Systems Medium Roads & Transport Planning

What does 'grade separation' mean in road engineering?

Using bridges, overpasses, or underpasses to allow two roads to cross at different levels without intersecting, eliminating traffic conflicts.

🚦 Infrastructure Systems Medium Railways & Tunnels

What is standard gauge in railway track, and what is its measurement?

Standard gauge is 1435 mm (4 ft 8½ in) — the distance between the inner faces of the two rails. It is used by most of the world's railways.

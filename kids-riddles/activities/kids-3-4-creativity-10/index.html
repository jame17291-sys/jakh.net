Ages 3–4 · Creativity & making

The dot that starts a journey

Draw a looping journey from a starting dot.

10 minRead with a grown-upPlay together

What we’re practising

Connect imagined places with a path that returns to its start.

Before you begin

Read the question aloud together. Use imagined people and places.

Let’s try it

  1. Draw a looping journey from a starting dot.
  2. Show or say your first answer before reading the example.
  3. Explain one choice, then try the extra challenge if you wish.

Talk about it

A dot is the start of a beetle’s walk. Where could your crayon take it before it comes home?

A little hint
  • Choose one stop and a way back.
Answer & what to notice

Example answer: My line visits a leaf, circles a stone and returns to the dot. Other reasoned ideas can work.

A drawn path can connect imagined places into a journey.

Make it easier

Visit just one place before returning.

Take it further

Add a stop where the beetle changes direction.

Away from the screen

Keep your drawing and ask a partner to invent a different solution to the same question.

What you’ll need

  • Paper
  • Large crayons

Use what you have

Describe or gesture your idea while a partner draws or writes.

Grown-up note

A grown-up reads and joins in; use intact drawing tools and let the child pause or pass.

Long and short paths

Compare two paths made from rolled towels.

10 minRead with a grown-upPlay together

What we’re practising

Compare lengths using a shared starting point.

Before you begin

Roll the towels and place one end of each at the same line.

Let’s try it

  1. Trace each path with a finger, starting at the shared line.
  2. Ask which reaches farther and name it longer.
  3. Move one towel forward, then realign the starts before comparing again.

Talk about it

Why should the paths start in the same place?

A little hint
  • Line up the near ends before looking at the far ends.
Answer & what to notice

The path extending farther from an aligned start is longer.

Moving an object changes its position, not its length.

Make it easier

Use paths with a large difference in length.

Take it further

Put three paths in order from shortest to longest.

Away from the screen

Compare two books’ heights by lining up their bottoms.

What you’ll need

  • Two towels of different lengths

Use what you have

Use two strips of paper laid flat.

Grown-up note

Stay with your child and use large, intact, age-appropriate materials.

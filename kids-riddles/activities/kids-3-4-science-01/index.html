Ages 3–4 · Science & nature

Hand shadow shapes

Change a hand shadow with a torch and wall.

10 minRead with a grown-upPlay together

What we’re practising

Observe how an object blocks light to make a shadow.

Before you begin

An adult points the torch at the wall in a dim, clear space.

Let’s try it

  1. Put a hand between the torch and wall and find its shadow.
  2. Move the hand closer to the torch while the torch stays still.
  3. Return it to the starting place and compare the shadow sizes.

Talk about it

What happened when your hand moved towards the light?

A little hint
  • Compare how much wall the shadow covers.
Answer & what to notice

With the wall and torch fixed, moving the hand towards the torch usually makes its shadow larger.

The hand blocks some light; its position changes the blocked area on the wall.

Make it easier

Watch one adult-made shadow and copy its hand shape.

Take it further

Predict which position makes the smaller shadow, then test.

Away from the screen

Trace an outdoor object’s shadow with your finger.

What you’ll need

  • Torch
  • Plain wall

Use what you have

Use daylight shadows on the ground; never look at the sun.

Grown-up note

An adult holds the torch; never shine it into eyes. Keep the floor clear.

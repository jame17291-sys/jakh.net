See the stem's support

What we’re practising

Identify a supporting plant structure.

Before you begin

Draw a sunflower with roots below, a stem, leaves and a flower above.

Let’s try it

  1. Draw a sunflower with roots below, a stem, leaves and a flower above.
  2. Ask: Which part holds the flower above the ground: the stem or the petals?
  3. Choose or draw an answer. Explain it using the clues; check the explanation together after trying.

Talk about it

Draw a sunflower with roots below, a stem, leaves and a flower above. Which part holds the flower above the ground: the stem or the petals?

A little hint
  • Follow the tall supporting part.
Answer & what to notice

The stem.

The stem supports the above-ground parts; petals are parts of the flower.

Make it easier

Trace the stem from soil to flower.

Take it further

Add leaves and show where they join the stem.

Away from the screen

Draw the idea as a two-picture explanation and tell someone which part is observed and which part is explained.

What you’ll need

  • Paper
  • Pencil or crayons

Use what you have

An adult can describe the scene aloud and accept a spoken or pointed answer. No printer is needed.

Grown-up note

An adult reads and draws with the child using age-appropriate drawing tools. Explore the situation in pictures.

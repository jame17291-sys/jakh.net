Kids Learning & Play / A recycled toy shelter

Ages 5–6 · Creativity & making

A recycled toy shelter

Design a small shelter that fits a toy.

30 minRead with a grown-upPlay together

What we’re practising

Plan, make and revise a structure for a stated purpose.

Before you begin

An adult removes staples and rough edges and cuts any needed doorway.

Let’s try it

  1. Place the toy beside the box and sketch a shelter with an entrance.
  2. Build walls or a roof using cardboard and tape.
  3. Test whether the toy fits and the entrance works; change one part if needed.

Talk about it

How will the toy enter without knocking the roof off?

A little hint
  • Compare the entrance with the toy’s widest part.
Answer & what to notice

A shelter succeeds if the toy fits and can enter; appearance can vary.

Testing against a purpose helps us decide what to improve.

Make it easier

Use an open-sided box and add a roof only.

Take it further

Add a second room with a connecting entrance.

Away from the screen

Invent a visitor and a story set in the shelter.

What you’ll need

  • Clean small cardboard box
  • Scrap cardboard
  • Paper tape
  • Small soft toy
  • Paper
  • Pencil

Use what you have

Use folded paper walls without tape.

Grown-up note

An adult handles all cutting and checks packaging for staples; no plastic bags, strings or sharp scraps.

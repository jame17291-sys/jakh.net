Kids Learning & Play / A picture revealed by a fold

Ages 5–6 · Creativity & making

A picture revealed by a fold

Fold paper once and design a bud outside with a surprise inside.

10 minRead with a grown-upPlay together

What we’re practising

Use a paper fold to control when a picture detail is revealed.

Before you begin

Read the question aloud together. Use imagined people and places.

Let’s try it

  1. Fold paper once and design a bud outside with a surprise inside.
  2. Show or say your first answer before reading the example.
  3. Explain one choice, then try the extra challenge if you wish.

Talk about it

A folded drawing shows only a small closed bud. What could appear when you open the paper?

A little hint
  • Decide what belongs outside and what stays hidden.
Answer & what to notice

Example answer: A large flower and visiting butterfly could be hidden inside the fold. Other reasoned ideas can work.

A fold can control which part of a picture the viewer sees first.

Make it easier

An adult folds; choose the hidden picture.

Take it further

Make the outside image give a small clue to the inside.

Away from the screen

Keep your drawing and ask a partner to invent a different solution to the same question.

What you’ll need

  • Paper
  • Large crayons

Use what you have

Describe or gesture your idea while a partner draws or writes.

Grown-up note

A grown-up reads and joins in; use intact drawing tools and let the child pause or pass.

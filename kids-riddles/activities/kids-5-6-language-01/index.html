Kids Learning & Play / First-sound picture hunt

Ages 5–6 · Language & stories

First-sound picture hunt

Group objects that begin with the same spoken sound.

10 minEarly readerPlay together

What we’re practising

Listen for initial sounds in familiar words.

Before you begin

Draw sun, sock and moon; say each word naturally.

Let’s try it

  1. Say “sun” and notice its first /s/ sound.
  2. Name the other pictures and choose one starting the same way.
  3. Draw another matching object, such as soap, and say its name.

Talk about it

Do sun and moon start with the same sound?

A little hint
  • Listen to the sound, not the letter’s alphabet name.
Answer & what to notice

Sun and sock start with /s/; moon starts with /m/. Soap begins with /s/.

Words can share an initial sound while having different meanings.

Make it easier

Offer only two pictures with clearly different starting sounds.

Take it further

Find three words beginning with another sound.

Away from the screen

Listen for the chosen sound in familiar object names.

What you’ll need

  • Paper
  • Crayons

Use what you have

Use spoken words without pictures.

Grown-up note

An adult reads or explains as needed and uses intact, age-appropriate materials.

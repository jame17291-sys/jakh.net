The toy train seats

Solve a position puzzle by elimination.

10 minEarly readerPlay together

What we’re practising

Solve a position puzzle by elimination.

Before you begin

Draw three seats in a row labelled first, middle, last.

Let’s try it

  1. Draw three seats in a row labelled first, middle, last.
  2. Ask: Cat sits first. Bear sits last. Rabbit takes the remaining seat. Where is Rabbit?
  3. Choose or draw an answer. Explain it using the clues; check the explanation together after trying.

Talk about it

Draw three seats in a row labelled first, middle, last. Cat sits first. Bear sits last. Rabbit takes the remaining seat. Where is Rabbit?

A little hint
  • Cross out the two occupied seats.
Answer & what to notice

Rabbit sits in the middle.

First and last are taken, leaving only middle.

Make it easier

Draw Cat and Bear in place.

Take it further

Swap Cat and Rabbit and describe all three positions.

Away from the screen

On another day, draw your own version of this challenge and explain which details must stay the same for the answer to work.

What you’ll need

  • Paper
  • Pencil or crayons

Use what you have

An adult can describe the scene aloud and accept a spoken or pointed answer. No printer is needed.

Grown-up note

An adult reads and draws with the child using age-appropriate drawing tools. Explore the situation in pictures.

Do the clues agree?

Notice contradictory conditions.

10 minEarly readerPlay together

What we’re practising

Notice contradictory conditions.

Before you begin

Draw one pretend toy described as “entirely red” and “entirely blue”, with red and blue distinct.

Let’s try it

  1. Draw one pretend toy described as “entirely red” and “entirely blue”, with red and blue distinct.
  2. Ask: Can both descriptions fit the same toy at the same time?
  3. Choose or draw an answer. Explain it using the clues; check the explanation together after trying.

Talk about it

Draw one pretend toy described as “entirely red” and “entirely blue”, with red and blue distinct. Can both descriptions fit the same toy at the same time?

A little hint
  • Entirely means every part.
Answer & what to notice

No, under these descriptions.

Every part cannot be exclusively red and exclusively blue at once.

Make it easier

Compare two solid-colour drawings.

Take it further

Change “entirely” to “partly”; draw a toy that now fits both.

Away from the screen

On another day, draw your own version of this challenge and explain which details must stay the same for the answer to work.

What you’ll need

  • Paper
  • Pencil or crayons

Use what you have

An adult can describe the scene aloud and accept a spoken or pointed answer. No printer is needed.

Grown-up note

An adult reads and draws with the child using age-appropriate drawing tools. Explore the situation in pictures.

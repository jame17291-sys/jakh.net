Kids Learning & Play / Tickets left in the strip

Ages 5–6 · Maths in everyday life

Tickets left in the strip

Talk through one short question together: Tickets left in the strip.

10 minEarly readerPlay together

What we’re practising

Subtract several items within ten.

Before you begin

An adult reads the whole question; pause to explain unfamiliar words.

Let’s try it

  1. Read the question below and identify what you need to find.
  2. Try an answer; show your thinking with words, fingers or a quick drawing.
  3. Use the hint if needed, then compare with the answer and explain why it fits.

Talk about it

A pretend ticket strip has eight tickets. Three are given out. How many remain?

A little hint
  • Start with eight and count back three steps.
Answer & what to notice

Five tickets.

Removing three from eight leaves five: 8 − 3 = 5.

Make it easier

Draw eight marks and cross out three.

Take it further

How many have been given out if only two remain?

Away from the screen

Another day, change one detail and explain whether the answer changes.

What you’ll need

  • No materials

Use what you have

Draw the scene or point to imagined choices if speaking is difficult.

Grown-up note

An adult stays with the child, reads aloud and accepts pointing or gestures.

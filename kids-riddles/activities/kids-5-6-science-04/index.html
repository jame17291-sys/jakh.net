Kids Learning & Play / Let the light through

Ages 5–6 · Science & nature

Let the light through

Compare clear, translucent and opaque materials.

15 minRead with a grown-upPlay together

What we’re practising

Observe that different materials transmit different amounts of light.

Before you begin

An adult holds the torch aimed at the wall at a fixed distance.

Let’s try it

  1. Hold the clear lid in the beam and observe the wall.
  2. Replace it with baking paper and then cardboard in the same position.
  3. Describe which lets a clear beam through, scatters it, or blocks it.

Talk about it

Which would make a better light-blocking cover?

A little hint
  • Compare the brightness on the wall rather than staring at the torch.
Answer & what to notice

Clear plastic transmits much light; baking paper scatters some; cardboard blocks most direct light.

The material affects how much light passes through and how it spreads.

Make it easier

Compare only clear plastic and cardboard.

Take it further

Predict what two layers of baking paper will do, then test.

Away from the screen

Find a window, curtain and wall and discuss their different jobs.

What you’ll need

  • Torch
  • Clear unbreakable lid
  • Baking paper
  • Cardboard
  • Wall

Use what you have

Use daylight at a window and look at objects through each material without looking at the sun.

Grown-up note

An adult controls the torch; never aim at eyes or look at the sun. Use no glass or plastic bags.

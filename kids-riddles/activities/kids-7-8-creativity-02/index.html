Rhythm conversation

Trade short sound patterns and build a shared rhythm.

15 minIndependent readerPlay together

What we’re practising

Create, repeat and deliberately vary a rhythmic pattern.

Before you begin

Agree on two gentle sounds, such as clap and knee tap.

Let’s try it

  1. Make a four-beat pattern, for example clap, tap, tap, rest.
  2. Have your partner repeat it, keeping the rest silent.
  3. Swap roles and change just one beat.
  4. Join the two patterns into an eight-beat conversation.

Talk about it

How does a rest change the pattern without adding a sound?

A little hint
  • Count four evenly spaced beats, including the quiet one.
Answer & what to notice

Any repeatable sequence works; the partner should preserve both sounds and rests.

Rhythm includes the timing of silence as well as sound.

Make it easier

Use a two-beat call and response.

Take it further

Keep the same rhythm but change which gentle sounds produce it.

Away from the screen

Give a familiar poem a gentle repeating beat.

What you’ll need

  • No materials

Use what you have

Use silent hand gestures or spoken syllables instead of claps.

Grown-up note

Keep sounds comfortable, stop if anyone dislikes them and avoid striking objects or people.

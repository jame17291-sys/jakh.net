Kids Learning & Play / A character shown by belongings

Ages 7–8 · Creativity & making

A character shown by belongings

Draw three belongings and explain the character they suggest.

15 minIndependent readerPlay together

What we’re practising

Suggest a character’s interests through selected belongings.

Before you begin

Read the question aloud together. Use imagined people and places.

Let’s try it

  1. Draw three belongings and explain the character they suggest.
  2. Show or say your first answer before reading the example.
  3. Explain one choice, then try the extra challenge if you wish.

Talk about it

Invent a character who enjoys exploring but dislikes carrying heavy things. What three belongings would help suggest this?

A little hint
  • Link each belonging to one part of the character description.
Answer & what to notice

Example answer: A folded map, a small notebook and a light bag could fit the character. Other reasoned ideas can work.

Selected props can imply interests and practical preferences without explaining everything.

Make it easier

Choose two of these imagined belongings: a light map, a tiny notebook, or a heavy stone.

Take it further

Add an unexpected belonging and invent a plausible reason for it.

Away from the screen

Keep your drawing and ask a partner to invent a different solution to the same question.

What you’ll need

  • Paper
  • Pencil

Use what you have

Describe or gesture your idea while a partner draws or writes.

Grown-up note

Keep examples fictional and participation voluntary; an adult can help read or act a role.

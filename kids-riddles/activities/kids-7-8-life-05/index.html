Kids Learning & Play / Make a shared-space checklist

Ages 7–8 · Together & everyday skills

Make a shared-space checklist

Create and test a short checklist for resetting a drawing area.

20 minIndependent readerPlay together

What we’re practising

Break a practical job into observable, manageable steps.

Before you begin

Choose a small safe area and agree what ready to use means.

Let’s try it

  1. List three visible actions, such as cap pens, stack paper and return pencils.
  2. Put the actions in a useful order and add checkboxes.
  3. Test the checklist together after a short drawing session.
  4. Change any instruction that was unclear or too large.

Talk about it

How will someone know each step is finished?

A little hint
  • Replace tidy up with a specific action such as put all pencils in the box.
Answer & what to notice

A useful checklist has clear actions and a visible finish; different spaces need different lists.

Small specific steps make a shared task easier to understand and check.

Make it easier

Use two picture steps with adult help.

Take it further

Ask another person to follow the list without extra explanation and revise it.

Away from the screen

Create a second checklist for putting away a board game.

What you’ll need

  • Paper
  • Pencils
  • A small drawing area

Use what you have

Use an imaginary desk drawn on paper.

Grown-up note

Adults handle cleaning chemicals, sharp items and heavy objects; this task uses only safe art materials.

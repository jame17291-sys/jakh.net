Louder is not higher

Distinguish pitch from loudness.

10 minIndependent readerGrown-up nearby

What we’re practising

Distinguish pitch from loudness.

Before you begin

Imagine the same musical note played softly and then loudly, without changing its pitch.

Let’s try it

  1. Imagine the same musical note played softly and then loudly, without changing its pitch.
  2. Ask: Which property changes: loudness or pitch?
  3. Choose or draw an answer. Explain it using the clues; check the explanation together after trying.

Talk about it

Imagine the same musical note played softly and then loudly, without changing its pitch. Which property changes: loudness or pitch?

A little hint
  • Pitch describes high or low notes; loudness describes strong or soft sound.
Answer & what to notice

Loudness changes; pitch stays the same in this example.

These are different sound properties, so one can change without the other.

Make it easier

Compare the words high/low and loud/soft.

Take it further

Give an example of a high, quiet sound and a low, loud sound.

Away from the screen

Draw the idea as a two-picture explanation and tell someone which part is observed and which part is explained.

What you’ll need

  • Paper
  • Pencil or crayons

Use what you have

An adult can describe the scene aloud and accept a spoken or pointed answer. No printer is needed.

Grown-up note

Explore the situation on paper with suitable drawing tools. Ask an adult before trying a real-world investigation.

Kids Learning & Play / A scene made of sounds

Ages 9–12 · Creativity & making

A scene made of sounds

Build a short imaginary setting using a sequence of gentle sounds.

20 minIndependent readerPlay together

What we’re practising

Select and arrange sound details to imply a setting and change.

Before you begin

Choose a setting such as a calm rainy garden, keeping the sound level comfortable.

Let’s try it

  1. List three possible sounds, such as fingertip rain, a soft bird whistle and a rustling hand motion.
  2. Arrange a beginning, a change and an ending using the sounds.
  3. Perform the scene for about thirty seconds without naming the setting.
  4. Ask a listener what they imagined, then revise one sound or pause.

Talk about it

Which sound suggested the setting most clearly?

A little hint
  • A gradual change from frequent taps to silence can suggest rain stopping.
Answer & what to notice

Different listeners may imagine different places; explain the intended clues and accept reasonable interpretations.

Sequence, loudness and silence can communicate change without spoken narration.

Make it easier

Use two sounds and let an adult narrate.

Take it further

Create two contrasting scenes using exactly the same three sound sources.

Away from the screen

Write a sound score with symbols another person could perform.

What you’ll need

  • No materials

Use what you have

Use written sound words or silent gestures instead.

Grown-up note

Avoid loud or sudden sounds and offer a silent version to anyone who prefers it.

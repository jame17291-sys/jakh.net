Ages 9–12 · Together & everyday skills

Plan a small shared event

Fit a welcome, activity and cleanup into a fixed fictional schedule.

20 minIndependent readerPlay together

What we’re practising

Allocate time, include a buffer and explain a scheduling choice.

Before you begin

An imaginary event runs 4:00–4:45 and needs 5 minutes welcome, 25 activity and 10 cleanup.

Let’s try it

  1. Add the required durations and find the time left over.
  2. Place the tasks in a sensible order and include the spare time as a buffer.
  3. Write start and end times for every block.
  4. Imagine the activity starts three minutes late and adjust without finishing after 4:45.

Talk about it

Why might a plan with every minute already occupied be harder to follow?

A little hint
  • Required tasks total 40 minutes, leaving 5 minutes free.
Answer & what to notice

One plan: welcome 4:00–4:05, activity 4:05–4:30, buffer 4:30–4:35, cleanup 4:35–4:45.

The five-minute buffer can absorb a three-minute delay while preserving all required durations and the finish time.

Make it easier

Place three prepared time blocks on a timeline.

Take it further

Add a second activity and decide what to shorten, explaining the tradeoff.

Away from the screen

Estimate and then observe the duration of one familiar household task.

What you’ll need

  • Paper
  • Pencil

Use what you have

Draw time blocks rather than a written timetable.

Grown-up note

The event is fictional; actual gatherings and responsibilities remain adult decisions.

Which notebook offer?

Compare bundle prices with both unit cost and a fixed spending limit.

15 minIndependent readerTry independently

What we’re practising

Calculate unit prices and distinguish cheapest per item from affordable today.

Before you begin

Write two offers: 3 notebooks for 12 tokens; 5 notebooks for 18 tokens. Budget: 15 tokens.

Let’s try it

  1. Calculate the price per notebook for each bundle.
  2. Identify which bundle has the lower unit price.
  3. Decide which complete bundle fits the budget; bundles cannot be split.
  4. Explain why the two answers differ.

Talk about it

Can the better unit price still be unaffordable?

A little hint
  • Divide the total price by the number of notebooks, then compare the full price with 15.
Answer & what to notice

The unit prices are 4 and 3.6 tokens. The five-pack is cheaper per notebook, but only the three-pack fits 15 tokens.

Unit price compares value for equal quantities; the total payment determines whether the budget covers the purchase.

Make it easier

Use 2 notebooks for 8 and 4 for 12.

Take it further

Find the lowest total cost to obtain at least eight notebooks using these whole bundles.

Away from the screen

Invent two offers where the larger bundle has a worse unit price.

What you’ll need

  • Paper
  • Pencil

Use what you have

Draw groups of notebooks and distribute pretend price tokens.

Grown-up note

Use fictional tokens; this activity requires no purchase or payment details.

A little curiosity, a world of discovery

Find a comfortable starting point

Use age as a guide, then follow what your child can enjoy and try.

Start with something familiar

Choose a short activity connected to something your child likes. Read the learning purpose and the reading-support label. Age bands are browsing guides, not tests or milestones; it is fine to explore the band before or after your child’s age.

Watch the first attempt

Try one step together. Can your child understand the task when you explain it? Can they make a choice or show an idea? Offer the easier variation if the instructions or materials get in the way. Reading aloud can remove a reading barrier without making the thinking easier.

Adjust one thing

Use fewer objects, give one instruction at a time, or show the first example. If the task is comfortable, ask “How do you know?” before moving to the harder version. Stop while there is still interest; completion is optional.

Try one small thing today

Choose one idea from this guide and one activity your child is curious about. Adapt it together, and keep what works for your family.

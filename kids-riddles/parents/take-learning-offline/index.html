Kids Learning & Play / Take the idea away from the screen

A little curiosity, a world of discovery

Take the idea away from the screen

Use the page to begin, then explore the room, the paper or the outdoors.

Read, then put the device aside

Read the steps and collect the materials before starting. Print the pack if helpful, or copy a short prompt onto paper. Most activities need no printer. Keep the device nearby only if you want to check a hint or explanation.

Find the same idea nearby

After a shape game, look for circles and rectangles in the room. After a story, retell it with paper characters. After a measurement activity, compare two familiar objects using the same unit. Use the activity’s offline extension for a specific next step.

Bring back a question

Ask “What did you notice?” or “What would you like to find out next?” A drawing, a spoken idea or a question is enough. Save a related activity for another day. Outdoor activities need an adult to choose a safe place and check the materials and surroundings.

Try one small thing today

Choose one idea from this guide and one activity your child is curious about. Adapt it together, and keep what works for your family.

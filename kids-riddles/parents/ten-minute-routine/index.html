Build a gentle ten-minute routine

A small invitation to play, with space for busy days.

Prepare less

Save two or three activities that use materials you already have. Place the paper or large blocks nearby before inviting your child. Choose a predictable moment that suits your family, such as after a snack. A routine can be occasional; it need not happen every day.

Invite, explore, reflect

Use the first minute to choose between two activities. Spend most of the time exploring one idea together. At the end ask “What would you try next time?” The suggested duration is an estimate, not a countdown. Pause if either of you needs a break.

Use the weekly plan flexibly

Add a ready-made week to the planner, then move activities to days that suit you. A blank day is fine. Mark completion only if it helps you remember what you tried. Repeat favourites and skip activities that do not fit this week.

Try one small thing today

Choose one idea from this guide and one activity your child is curious about. Adapt it together, and keep what works for your family.

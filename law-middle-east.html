⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Islamic Jurisprudence & Sources

What is the Arabic term for Islamic law derived from the Quran and Sunnah?

Sharia (الشريعة).

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Islamic Jurisprudence & Sources

What is fiqh in Islamic legal terminology?

Fiqh is Islamic jurisprudence — the practical rulings derived by scholars from the sources of Sharia.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Islamic Jurisprudence & Sources

What are the four main sources of Islamic law (usul al-fiqh)?

The Quran, the Sunnah (Hadith), Ijma (scholarly consensus), and Qiyas (analogical reasoning).

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Islamic Jurisprudence & Sources

What is a fatwa in Islamic law?

A formal legal opinion or ruling issued by a qualified Islamic scholar (mufti) on a religious or legal question.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Constitutional & Legal Systems

What legal system is based on the Napoleonic Code and is used in many Arab countries?

Civil law (code law) system, as inherited from French or Egyptian civil codes.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Criminal Law & Justice

What is the Arabic term for the fixed punishments prescribed by Islamic law for specific offenses?

Hudud (حدود) — the set of specific punishments for crimes like theft, slander, and highway robbery.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Criminal Law & Justice

What is the Islamic legal principle of qisas?

Qisas is the principle of proportional retaliation ('an eye for an eye') in cases of murder or bodily harm, where the victim's family may choose retaliation, blood money, or forgiveness.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Family & Inheritance Law

What is the Arabic legal term for a Muslim marriage contract?

Aqd al-nikah (عقد النكاح) — the marriage contract requiring offer, acceptance, two witnesses, and a dowry (mahr).

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Family & Inheritance Law

What is the mahr in Islamic marriage law?

A mandatory gift or payment made by the groom to the bride, which becomes her exclusive property.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Family & Inheritance Law

What is the Arabic term for the waiting period a woman must observe after divorce or a husband's death before remarrying?

Iddah (عدة) — typically three menstrual cycles after divorce, or four months and ten days after a husband's death.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Civil, Property & Labor Law

What is a waqf in Islamic law?

A waqf is a religious endowment — property donated for charitable, religious, or educational purposes, which cannot be sold or transferred.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Family & Inheritance Law

What is the science of Islamic inheritance law called?

Ilm al-faraidh (علم الفرائض) — the science of prescribed inheritance shares defined in the Quran.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy International Law & Human Rights

What does the term 'sovereignty' mean in international law?

The exclusive authority of a state to govern itself, make laws, and conduct affairs within its territory without interference from other states.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Criminal Law & Justice

What does the Latin phrase 'presumption of innocence' mean in criminal law?

A defendant is considered innocent until proven guilty beyond reasonable doubt — the prosecution bears the burden of proof.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Constitutional & Legal Systems

What country has the largest and most influential civil code in the Arab world, widely adopted by other nations?

Egypt — the Egyptian Civil Code (1949), drafted by Abd El-Razzak El-Sanhuri, was adopted as the basis for civil law in Iraq, Syria, Libya, Kuwait, and others.

⚖️ Middle East Law Easy Islamic Jurisprudence & Sources

What are the five categories of acts in Islamic law (ahkam al-khamsa)?

Wajib (obligatory), Mandub (recommended), Mubah (permissible), Makruh (discouraged), and Haram (forbidden).

⚖️ Middle East Law Medium Islamic Jurisprudence & Sources

What are the four major Sunni schools of Islamic jurisprudence (madhabs)?

The Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali schools, each named after its founding scholar.

⚖️ Middle East Law Medium Civil, Property & Labor Law

What are the essential elements of a valid contract in most Arab civil law systems?

Offer and acceptance (meeting of minds), legal capacity of the parties, a lawful object, and a lawful cause (consideration).

⚖️ Middle East Law Medium International Law & Human Rights

What is the Arab League and what legal role does it play in the region?

Founded in 1945, the Arab League is a regional organization of Arab states that promotes cooperation and coordinates policy. It adopts resolutions and conventions, though its legal instruments are generally non-binding on member states without domestic ratification.

⚖️ Middle East Law Hard Constitutional & Legal Systems

What constitutional provision is common to Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and the UAE regarding the basis of their legal systems?

All three designate Islamic Sharia as a (or the) principal source of legislation in their constitutional documents.

01A lily pad doubles in size every day. It takes 48 days to cover the entire pond. How long did it take to cover half the pond?

Answer

47 days. On day 47 it covered half — then doubled on day 48 to cover it all.

04Lina, Sami, and Noor each chose a different drink: tea, coffee, or juice. Sami chose coffee, Noor did not choose juice, and Lina did not choose tea. Who chose each drink?

Answer

Noor chose tea, Sami chose coffee, and Lina chose juice. Once coffee is assigned to Sami, Noor's only allowed drink is tea, leaving juice for Lina.

13A three-digit vault code has distinct digits. The first digit is twice the third, the middle digit is one greater than the third, and all three digits add to 13. What is the code?

Answer

643. Let the third digit be t. The digits are 2t, t+1, and t, so 4t+1=13 and t=3.

Answer

18A treasure is in exactly one of three boxes. Box 1 says, “The treasure is in Box 2.” Box 2 says, “The treasure is not in Box 2.” Box 3 says, “The treasure is not in Box 1.” Exactly one note is true. Which box holds the treasure?

Answer

19Four cards show A, D, 4 and 7. Each has an English letter on one side and a number on the other. To test “If the letter is a vowel (A, E, I, O or U), the number is even,” which cards must you turn over?

22A car is placed uniformly behind one of three doors. You choose one. The host knows its location, always opens an unchosen goat door, and always offers a switch to the other closed door. Does always switching or always staying win more often?

Answer

Switching wins with probability 2/3; staying wins with probability 1/3. Switching wins exactly when the initial choice was wrong.

Answer

24Exactly one of two paths is safe. Two guards know which one: one always answers truthfully and the other always lies. Both know the other’s rule. What single yes/no question to either guard lets you choose the safe path?

Answer

Ask: “Would the other guard say the left path is safe?” If yes, go right; if no, go left. Either guard gives the opposite of the truth about the left path.

26A snail is at the bottom of a 10-metre well. Each day it climbs 3 metres and each night slips back 2 metres. How many days does it take to escape?

Answer

8 days. After seven full days and nights, the snail is at 7 m. On day 8 it climbs to 10 m and escapes, so it does not slide back that night.

27Three switches outside a room control three initially off incandescent bulbs inside. The bulbs become warm when on and remain detectably warm briefly after switching off. You may safely inspect light and residual warmth, but enter only once. How can you match switches to bulbs?

Answer

Leave switch 1 on long enough to warm its bulb, then turn it off. Turn switch 2 on and enter promptly. Lit = 2; unlit but warm = 1; unlit and cool = 3.

29You have two ropes, each taking exactly 1 hour to burn, but with non-uniform burning rates. How do you measure exactly 45 minutes?

Answer

Light rope 1 at both ends and rope 2 at one end simultaneously. When rope 1 finishes (30 min), light rope 2's other end. When rope 2 finishes = 45 minutes total.

30What is the last digit of 7¹⁰⁰?

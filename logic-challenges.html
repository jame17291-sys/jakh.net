01A lily pad doubles in size every day. It takes 48 days to cover the entire pond. How long did it take to cover half the pond?

Answer

47 days. On day 47 it covered half — then doubled on day 48 to cover it all.

02A girl has as many brothers as sisters. Each brother has half as many brothers as sisters. How many children are there in total?

Answer

7 children: 4 girls and 3 boys. Each girl has 3 brothers and 3 sisters. Each boy has 2 brothers and 4 sisters.

03You overtake the person in 2nd place in a race. What place are you in now?

Answer

2nd place. You took their position — you did not overtake 1st place.

04In this puzzle, each egg needs exactly five minutes once placed in boiling water. A large pot keeps boiling normally with five eggs in it. If all five go in together, when are they ready?

Answer

After five minutes: the cooking happens simultaneously.

05Lina, Sami, and Noor each chose a different drink: tea, coffee, or juice. Sami chose coffee, Noor did not choose juice, and Lina did not choose tea. Who chose each drink?

Answer

Noor chose tea, Sami chose coffee, and Lina chose juice. Once coffee is assigned to Sami, Noor's only allowed drink is tea, leaving juice for Lina.

06Three books—red, green, and blue—stand in a row. The red book is to the left of the green book, and the blue book is to the right of the green book. What is their order from left to right?

Answer

Red, green, blue.

07A three-digit vault code has distinct digits. The first digit is twice the third, the middle digit is one greater than the third, and all three digits add to 13. What is the code?

Answer

643. Let the third digit be t. The digits are 2t, t+1, and t, so 4t+1=13 and t=3.

08What is the sum of all integers from 1 to 100?

Answer

5,050. Gauss's formula: n(n+1)/2 = 100 × 101 / 2 = 5,050.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Easy Lateral Thinking

A lily pad doubles in size every day. It takes 48 days to cover the entire pond. How long did it take to cover half the pond?

47 days. On day 47 it covered half — then doubled on day 48 to cover it all.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Easy Logic & Deduction

A girl has as many brothers as sisters. Each brother has half as many brothers as sisters. How many children are there in total?

7 children: 4 girls and 3 boys. Each girl has 3 brothers and 3 sisters. Each boy has 2 brothers and 4 sisters.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Easy Lateral Thinking

You overtake the person in 2nd place in a race. What place are you in now?

2nd place. You took their position — you did not overtake 1st place.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Easy Lateral Thinking

If it takes 5 minutes to boil one egg, how long does it take to boil 5 eggs together?

5 minutes. All eggs boil simultaneously in the same pot.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Easy Logic & Deduction

Lina, Sami, and Noor each chose a different drink: tea, coffee, or juice. Sami chose coffee, Noor did not choose juice, and Lina did not choose tea. Who chose each drink?

Noor chose tea, Sami chose coffee, and Lina chose juice. Once coffee is assigned to Sami, Noor's only allowed drink is tea, leaving juice for Lina.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Easy Constraints & Ordering

Three books—red, green, and blue—stand in a row. The red book is to the left of the green book, and the blue book is to the right of the green book. What is their order from left to right?

Red, green, blue.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Easy Numbers & Patterns

When I was 6 my sister was half my age. Now I am 70. How old is my sister?

67. She was 3 when I was 6 — so she is always 3 years younger than me.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Medium Numbers & Patterns

A two-digit number has digits that add to 11. Reversing its digits makes the number 27 greater. What is the original number?

47. If the tens digit is a and the ones digit is b, then a+b=11 and 9(b−a)=27, so b−a=3. Thus a=4 and b=7.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Medium Constraints & Ordering

A three-digit vault code has distinct digits. The first digit is twice the third, the middle digit is one greater than the third, and all three digits add to 13. What is the code?

643. Let the third digit be t. The digits are 2t, t+1, and t, so 4t+1=13 and t=3.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Medium Lateral Thinking

Is 8% of 50 the same as 50% of 8?

Yes, both equal 4. In general, x% of y always equals y% of x.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Medium Numbers & Patterns

What is the sum of all integers from 1 to 100?

5,050. Gauss's formula: n(n+1)/2 = 100 × 101 / 2 = 5,050.

🧠 Logic Puzzles Medium Numbers & Patterns

A mix contains 3 parts water and 1 part juice. How much juice is in a 12-litre mix?

3 litres. Juice is 1/4 of total: 12 × 1/4 = 3.

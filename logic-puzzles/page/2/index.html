21You drive to a shop at 20 mph and return at 60 mph. What is your average speed for the whole trip?

Answer

30 mph. Use the harmonic mean for equal distances: 2 × 20 × 60 / (20 + 60) = 2400 / 80 = 30.

22What is the smallest perfect number (a number equal to the sum of its proper divisors)?

Answer

6. Its proper divisors are 1, 2, and 3, and 1 + 2 + 3 = 6.

23If a man's only son is my son's father, who am I to the man?

Answer

His son. The man's only son is my son's father — meaning I am that son.

24A treasure is in exactly one of three boxes. Box 1 says, “The treasure is in Box 2.” Box 2 says, “The treasure is not in Box 2.” Box 3 says, “The treasure is not in Box 1.” Exactly one note is true. Which box holds the treasure?

Answer

Box 1. If the treasure is there, only Box 2's note is true. Putting it in Box 2 or Box 3 makes two notes true.

29Four cards show A, D, 4, and 7. Each card has a letter on one side and a number on the other. To test the rule “If a card has a vowel on one side, then it has an even number on the other,” which cards must you turn over?

Answer

A and 7. Turn A to check for an even number, and 7 to make sure there is not a vowel on its other side. D and 4 cannot disprove the rule.

32A cube is painted on all six faces and cut into 27 equal smaller cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two painted faces?

Answer

12. Each of the cube's 12 edges has one non-corner small cube, and each such cube has exactly two painted faces.

35Exactly two of four lamps A, B, C, and D are lit. A and B are in opposite states, C is in the same state as A, and D is off. Which lamps are lit?

Answer

A and C. If A were off, only B would be lit, contradicting the condition that exactly two lamps are lit. Therefore A and C are on, while B and D are off.

36The Monty Hall Problem: You pick 1 of 3 doors. A car is behind one. The host opens a different door showing a goat and offers you a switch. Should you switch?

Answer

Yes — switch! Switching wins 2/3 of the time. Your original pick is wrong 2/3 of the time, and the host's reveal concentrates that probability onto the remaining door.

37100 lockers are all closed. Person 1 opens all; person 2 toggles every 2nd; person 3 toggles every 3rd... Which lockers remain open after all 100 people?

Answer

The perfect squares: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100. Only perfect squares have an odd number of divisors, so they end up open.

38You have two children and at least one is a boy. What is the probability that both are boys?

Answer

1/3. Possible pairs with at least one boy: BB, BG, GB — only 1 of 3 outcomes is BB.

39What does the infinite series 1 + 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + ... converge to?

Answer

2. It is a geometric series with first term 1 and ratio 1/2: sum = 1 / (1 − 1/2) = 2.

40An ant sits on one corner of a 1×1×1 cube. What is the shortest path along the surface to the opposite corner?

Answer

√5 ≈ 2.236. Unfold two faces into a flat 1×2 rectangle — the straight line across is √(1² + 2²) = √5.

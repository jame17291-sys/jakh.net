01A bat and a ball cost $1.10 total. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?

Answer

$0.05. The ball is 5 cents, the bat is $1.05, and together they equal $1.10.

15Three men pay $10 each ($30). The manager refunds $5 via a bellboy who keeps $2 and returns $1 to each man. They each paid $9 net ($27 total). $27 + $2 = $29. Where is the missing dollar?

Answer

There is no missing dollar. The men paid $27 in total ($25 room + $2 bellboy tip). Adding the $2 tip to the $27 is a trick — the $2 is already inside the $27, not outside it. Correct accounting: $30 paid − $3 refunded = $27 spent, split as $25 (hotel) + $2 (bellboy).

Answer

30Three ants sit at the corners of a triangle. Each picks one of its two edges at random and walks along it at the same speed. What is the chance that no two ants meet?

Answer

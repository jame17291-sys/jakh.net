24Three men pay $10 each ($30). The manager refunds $5 via a bellboy who keeps $2 and returns $1 to each man. They each paid $9 net ($27 total). $27 + $2 = $29. Where is the missing dollar?

Answer

There is no missing dollar. The men paid $27 in total ($25 room + $2 bellboy tip). Adding the $2 tip to the $27 is a trick — the $2 is already inside the $27, not outside it. Correct accounting: $30 paid − $3 refunded = $27 spent, split as $25 (hotel) + $2 (bellboy).

53One of two boxes is chosen at random. Box A contains 2 gold coins and 1 silver coin; Box B contains 1 gold coin and 2 silver coins. A gold coin is drawn. What is the probability that Box A was chosen?

Answer

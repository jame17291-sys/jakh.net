∑ Math Easy Logic, Foundations & Unsolved Problems

A bat and a ball cost $1.10 total. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?

$0.05. The ball is 5 cents, the bat is $1.05, and together they equal $1.10.

∑ Math Easy Patterns, Sequences & Word Problems

If you are driving at 60 mph, how long does it take to travel 60 miles?

1 hour. Distance ÷ Speed = 60 ÷ 60 = 1 hour.

∑ Math Easy Patterns, Sequences & Word Problems

On a map, 1 cm represents 5 km. Two towns are 7 cm apart on the map. What is the actual distance between them?

35 km. Multiply the map distance by the scale: 7 × 5 = 35.

∑ Math Easy Number Theory & Number Riddles

Which is greater: 5/8 or 3/5?

5/8. Using a common denominator, 5/8 = 25/40 while 3/5 = 24/40.

∑ Math Easy Patterns, Sequences & Word Problems

A farmer has 10 cows and 10 chickens. How many legs are there in total?

60 legs. Cows: 4×10=40; chickens: 2×10=20. Total = 60.

∑ Math Easy Geometry & Trigonometry

At 3:00, what is the angle between the hour and minute hand?

90°. The minute hand is at 12, the hour hand is at 3 — one quarter of 360°.

∑ Math Easy Algebra & Functions

What is (-1)⁴?

1. Negative × Negative = Positive, applied twice: (-1)(-1)(-1)(-1) = 1.

∑ Math Easy Logic, Foundations & Unsolved Problems

A baker has 13 loaves of bread. He sells all but 9. How many loaves are left?

9. 'All but 9' means 9 remain — it's a reading trick, not arithmetic.

∑ Math Easy Geometry & Trigonometry

What is the reflection of the point (3, −2) across the x-axis?

(3, 2). Reflection across the x-axis keeps the x-coordinate and changes the sign of the y-coordinate.

∑ Math Easy Number Theory & Number Riddles

How many years are in a millennium?

1,000 years. 'Milli-' means thousand.

∑ Math Easy Number Theory & Number Riddles

How many items are in a baker's dozen?

13. Bakers traditionally added an extra item to a dozen (12) as a generous bonus.

∑ Math Easy Number Theory & Number Riddles

What is the square root of 144?

12. Because 12 × 12 = 144.

∑ Math Easy Number Theory & Number Riddles

What number is represented by the Roman numeral 'LXVI'?

66. L=50, X=10, V=5, I=1. So 50+10+5+1=66.

∑ Math Easy Number Theory & Number Riddles

What is the decimal value of the binary number 101?

5. In binary: 1×4 + 0×2 + 1×1 = 5.

∑ Math Easy Geometry & Trigonometry

How many lines of symmetry does a circle have?

Infinite. Any line through the centre is a line of symmetry.

∑ Math Easy Patterns, Sequences & Word Problems

What is the next number in the sequence 2, 6, 12, 20, ...?

30. The terms follow n(n+1): 1×2, 2×3, 3×4, 4×5, so the next is 5×6.

∑ Math Easy Number Theory & Number Riddles

Is 1,234,567,890 divisible by 9?

Yes. The sum of its digits is 1+2+3+4+5+6+7+8+9+0 = 45, which is divisible by 9.

∑ Math Easy Probability, Combinatorics & Statistics

What is the mean (average) of 10, 20, and 60?

30. Add them: 10+20+60=90, then divide by 3: 90÷3=30.

∑ Math Easy Geometry & Trigonometry

What is the sum of the interior angles of a triangle?

180°. This is a fundamental theorem of Euclidean geometry.

∑ Math Easy Patterns, Sequences & Word Problems

Three notebooks cost $4 each. If you pay with $20, how much change should you receive?

$8. The notebooks cost 3 × $4 = $12, so the change is $20 − $12 = $8.

62A tank is 3/5 full. After 24 litres are added, it is 9/10 full. What is the tank's capacity?

Answer

80 litres. The added water is 9/10 − 3/5 = 3/10 of the capacity, so the capacity is 24 ÷ (3/10) = 80.

68One of two boxes is chosen at random. Box A contains 2 gold coins and 1 silver coin; Box B contains 1 gold coin and 2 silver coins. A gold coin is drawn. What is the probability that Box A was chosen?

Answer

2/3. By Bayes' rule, P(A and gold) = (1/2)(2/3) = 1/3, while P(gold) = 1/2, so P(A | gold) = (1/3)/(1/2) = 2/3.

71A regular hexagon is inscribed in a circle of radius 4. What is the exact area inside the circle but outside the hexagon?

Answer

16π − 24√3 square units. An inscribed regular hexagon has side length equal to the radius, so its area is 6 × (√3/4) × 4² = 24√3; subtract this from the circle's area 16π.

72How many distinct arrangements of all the letters of BANANA have no two A's adjacent?

Answer

12. Arrange B, N, N in 3!/2! = 3 ways. They create four gaps; choose three of those gaps for the identical A's, giving C(4,3) = 4 choices. Thus 3 × 4 = 12.

77If r and s are the roots of x² − 7x + 10 = 0, what is 1/r + 1/s?

Answer

7/10. By Vieta's formulas, r + s = 7 and rs = 10, so 1/r + 1/s = (r+s)/(rs) = 7/10.

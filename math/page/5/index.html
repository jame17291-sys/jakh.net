81A sequence is defined by a₁ = 1 and aₙ₊₁ = √(2 + aₙ). If the sequence converges, what is its limit?

Answer

2. If the limit is L, then L = √(2+L), so L² − L − 2 = 0. The roots are 2 and −1, and the sequence is positive, so L = 2.

83What is the general form of a Mersenne prime?

Answer

2^p − 1, where p is itself prime. Examples: 3 (2²−1), 7 (2³−1), 31 (2⁵−1).

92Two trains 60 miles apart head toward each other at 30 mph. A fly travels between them at 60 mph. How far does the fly travel before the trains collide?

Answer

60 miles. The trains take 1 hour to meet; the fly covers 60 mph × 1 hour = 60 miles. No infinite series needed.

81What is the mechanism behind long-QT syndrome?

Answer

Prolonged cardiac repolarisation (due to potassium channel mutations or drugs) delays the cardiac action potential, increasing risk of torsades de pointes arrhythmia

82What is checkpoint immunotherapy?

Answer

Drugs targeting immune checkpoint proteins (PD-1, PD-L1, CTLA-4) to 'release the brakes' on T cells, allowing them to attack cancer cells

Answer

98What is the pathophysiology of ARDS (Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome)?

Answer

Widespread lung inflammation → increased capillary permeability → non-cardiogenic pulmonary oedema → impaired gas exchange → hypoxaemia

Answer

The production of blood cells from haematopoietic stem cells — primarily in red bone marrow of flat bones (sternum, iliac crest, vertebrae, ribs) in adults

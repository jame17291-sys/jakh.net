🕌 Middle East History Easy Ancient

Which ancient civilization built the pyramids at Giza?

The ancient Egyptians, during the Old Kingdom period (c. 2580–2560 BC).

🕌 Middle East History Easy Ancient

What ancient civilization of Mesopotamia is credited with creating the world's earliest known writing system?

The Sumerians, who developed cuneiform writing around 3200 BC.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Ancient

What ancient empire was based in Persia and at its peak stretched from Egypt to India?

The Achaemenid (Persian) Empire, founded by Cyrus the Great in 550 BC.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Islamic History

In which city was Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) born?

Mecca (Makkah), in the Arabian Peninsula, around 570 CE.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Islamic History

What year did the Prophet Muhammad migrate from Mecca to Medina, marking the start of the Islamic calendar?

622 CE — the Hijra. The Islamic calendar (AH) is dated from this event.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Islamic History

What was the first Islamic caliphate, and who were its first four caliphs?

The Rashidun Caliphate (632–661 CE). The four caliphs were Abu Bakr al-Siddiq, Umar ibn al-Khattab, Uthman ibn Affan, and Ali ibn Abi Talib.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Islamic History

What dynasty built its capital in Damascus and ruled the early Islamic world from 661 to 750 CE?

The Umayyad Caliphate.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Islamic History

Which Islamic caliphate succeeded the Umayyads and made Baghdad its capital?

The Abbasid Caliphate (750–1258 CE).

🕌 Middle East History Easy Islamic History

What was the Bayt al-Hikma (House of Wisdom) in Baghdad?

A major intellectual and scholarly center in Abbasid Baghdad, where scholars translated Greek, Persian, and Indian texts into Arabic and produced advances in mathematics, medicine, astronomy, and philosophy during the Islamic Golden Age.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Islamic History

Who was Saladin and what is he best known for in history?

Saladin (Salah al-Din al-Ayyubi) was the Muslim Kurdish leader who founded the Ayyubid dynasty and is most famous for recapturing Jerusalem from the Crusaders in 1187.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Ottoman History

What empire ruled much of the Middle East and North Africa for about 400 years until World War I?

The Ottoman Empire, centered in Constantinople (Istanbul).

🕌 Middle East History Easy Ottoman History

What was the year the Ottoman Empire ended and was replaced by the Republic of Turkey?

1923 — the Republic of Turkey was proclaimed on October 29, 1923, by Mustafa Kemal Ataturk.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Modern History

What document issued in 1917 by Britain expressed support for a Jewish homeland in Palestine?

The Balfour Declaration, issued by British Foreign Secretary Arthur Balfour on November 2, 1917.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Modern History

In what year was the State of Israel declared?

1948 — declared on May 14, 1948, by David Ben-Gurion.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Modern History

What was the Suez Crisis of 1956?

Egypt's President Nasser nationalized the Suez Canal, prompting a military attack by Britain, France, and Israel. US and Soviet pressure forced them to withdraw, marking a major shift in post-colonial power and Egypt's rise as a regional leader.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Modern History

What was the Arab Spring?

A wave of pro-democracy protests and uprisings that swept the Arab world from late 2010, toppling governments in Tunisia, Egypt, Libya, and Yemen, and triggering civil wars in Syria and elsewhere.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Modern History

In which year did Iraq invade Kuwait, triggering the Gulf War?

1990 — Iraq invaded Kuwait on August 2, 1990. The US-led coalition liberated Kuwait in early 1991.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Ancient

What ancient city is considered one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, located in Syria?

Damascus — inhabited for at least 11,000 years and considered one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Modern History

What country did the United States and a coalition invade in 2003, toppling the government?

Iraq — the 2003 invasion led to the fall of Saddam Hussein's government.

🕌 Middle East History Easy Modern History

In what year did Egypt and Israel sign a historic peace treaty?

1979 — the Egypt-Israel Peace Treaty was signed on March 26, 1979, making Egypt the first Arab country to formally recognize Israel.

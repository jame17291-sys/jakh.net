Panic Mode: 5 Seconds

Think fast. Say three answers before the timer disappears. Your friends decide whether you nailed it.

Free · No account needed

How to play

Pass the phone to the player. Start the timer to reveal the challenge and say three answers aloud within five seconds. The group decides whether the answers are valid: success earns 1 point; failure or skipping earns none.

Keep it kind: anyone can skip a round at any time.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy Mechanics, Energy & Relativity

What is the speed of light in a vacuum?

Approximately 300,000 km/s (299,792,458 m/s).

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy Cell, Genetics & Microbiology

What region of a bacterial cell contains its chromosome?

The nucleoid. Unlike a eukaryotic nucleus, it is not enclosed by a membrane.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy General Chemistry

In an aqueous solution, which substance is the solvent?

Water. The term aqueous means that a substance is dissolved in water.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy Earth, Plant & Human Sciences

Which blood components form the initial plug that helps stop bleeding?

Platelets. They adhere to damaged blood vessels and aggregate to form a platelet plug.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy Waves & Optics

How are the frequency f and period T of a repeating wave related?

They are reciprocals: f = 1/T. A longer period therefore means a lower frequency.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy General Chemistry

What is the most abundant gas in Earth's atmosphere?

Nitrogen (about 78%).

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy Cell, Genetics & Microbiology

What molecule carries genetic information in living cells?

DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid).

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy Waves & Optics

Which type of lens brings parallel light rays to a focus?

A convex, or converging, lens.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy General Chemistry

What is the pH of a neutral solution at 25 °C?

7.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy Earth, Plant & Human Sciences

How do root hairs help a plant absorb water and minerals?

They greatly increase the root's surface area in contact with the soil.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy Mechanics, Energy & Relativity

According to Newton's first law, what happens to an object at rest if no net force acts on it?

It remains at rest (law of inertia).

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy Earth, Plant & Human Sciences

What property of minerals is ranked by the Mohs scale?

Scratch hardness: resistance to being scratched by another material.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Easy Cell, Genetics & Microbiology

Which molecule serves as the immediate energy currency of cells?

ATP (adenosine triphosphate). Cells release usable energy when ATP is hydrolyzed.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Medium Mechanics, Energy & Relativity

What does Einstein's famous equation E = mc² represent?

Energy equals mass times the speed of light squared — mass and energy are equivalent and interconvertible.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Medium General Chemistry

What is Avogadro's number and what does it represent?

6.022 × 10²³; it is the number of atoms, molecules, or ions in one mole of a substance.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Medium Cell, Genetics & Microbiology

What is the difference between mitosis and meiosis?

Mitosis produces two genetically identical diploid cells for growth and repair; meiosis produces four genetically unique haploid cells for sexual reproduction.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Medium Waves & Optics

What is the Doppler effect?

The change in observed frequency of a wave when the source or observer is moving; e.g., a siren sounds higher-pitched as it approaches and lower as it recedes.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Medium General Chemistry

What is an exothermic reaction?

A reaction that releases heat energy to the surroundings, resulting in a negative ΔH.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Medium Earth, Plant & Human Sciences

What is natural selection?

The process by which organisms with traits better suited to their environment survive and reproduce more successfully, driving evolution over generations.

🔬 Physical & Life Sciences Medium Mechanics, Energy & Relativity

What is the difference between a scalar and a vector quantity?

A scalar has only magnitude (e.g., speed, temperature), while a vector has both magnitude and direction (e.g., velocity, force).

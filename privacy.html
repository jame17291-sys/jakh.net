Privacy

Privacy at JAKH

We keep this page simple: what we collect, why we use it, how long we keep it, and how to contact us.

Privacy contact info@jakh.net

What JAKH collects

When you create an account, we may store your username, password hash, saved progress, favorites, game scores, and basic account settings. When you play online rooms, we process room codes, game state, and connection events needed to run the game.

How we use data

We use data to sign you in, save progress, operate leaderboards, run multiplayer games, improve the website, prevent abuse, and respond to messages sent to info@jakh.net.

Analytics and cookies

JAKH may use essential cookies for sign-in and site functionality. We may also use privacy-conscious analytics to understand which pages work well. Analytics should not replace a direct support request if you need help.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use infrastructure providers, analytics providers, or security tools only when needed to operate and protect the website.

Retention

Account and progress data is kept while your account is active. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle the request and maintain a useful record.

Your choices

You can play many parts of JAKH without an account. You can request access, correction, export, or deletion of account data by emailing info@jakh.net.

Children and families

JAKH includes learning and riddle content, but it is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13. Parents or guardians can contact us at info@jakh.net for account or data requests.

Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards such as HTTPS, hashed passwords, access controls, and operational monitoring. No online service can guarantee perfect security, so please use a strong password.

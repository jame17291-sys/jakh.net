💞 Relationships Easy Communication

What percentage of human communication is estimated to be nonverbal (body language, tone, etc.)?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Research suggests over 70% of communication is nonverbal, including body language, facial expressions, and tone of voice.

💞 Relationships Easy Love & Attachment

According to Sternberg's Triangular Theory of Love, what are the three components of love?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Intimacy, passion, and commitment.

💞 Relationships Easy Psychology

What is 'active listening'?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Fully concentrating on what the other person is saying, understanding their message, responding thoughtfully, and remembering what was said — rather than passively hearing words.

💞 Relationships Easy Conflict & Resolution

What is the 'silent treatment' and why do psychologists consider it harmful in relationships?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Deliberately ignoring or refusing to communicate with a partner as a form of punishment. It is harmful because it blocks resolution, creates emotional distance, and is a form of emotional manipulation.

💞 Relationships Easy Love & Attachment

What are John Gottman's 'Four Horsemen' — the four negative communication patterns that predict relationship breakdown?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Criticism, contempt, defensiveness, and stonewalling.

💞 Relationships Easy Psychology

What is empathy and how does it differ from sympathy?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Empathy is feeling with someone — stepping into their emotional experience. Sympathy is feeling for someone from a distance, acknowledging their feelings without sharing them.

💞 Relationships Easy Communication

What does it mean to use 'I statements' in a conflict?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Expressing feelings from your own perspective ('I feel hurt when...') rather than blaming the other person ('You always...'). It reduces defensiveness and opens dialogue.

💞 Relationships Easy Love & Attachment

What are Gary Chapman's 'Five Love Languages'?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Words of Affirmation, Acts of Service, Receiving Gifts, Quality Time, and Physical Touch.

💞 Relationships Easy Psychology

What is 'gaslighting' in the context of relationships?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

A form of psychological manipulation where one person causes another to question their own memory, perception, or sanity — e.g., denying events that occurred or telling the victim they are 'overreacting'.

💞 Relationships Easy Communication

What is the 'sandwich' feedback method?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Delivering criticism between two positive statements: start with a compliment, deliver the criticism, end with encouragement. It softens the impact of negative feedback.

💞 Relationships Easy Love & Attachment

What is the 'proximity effect' (mere exposure effect) in attraction?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

People tend to develop positive feelings toward things or people they are repeatedly exposed to simply due to familiarity — one reason relationships often form between neighbours or coworkers.

💞 Relationships Easy Psychology

What does 'codependency' mean in a relationship?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

A pattern where one person excessively relies on the other for emotional validation and identity, often enabling unhealthy behaviours. One partner's needs consistently come before their own well-being.

💞 Relationships Easy Communication

What is 'reflective listening'?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Paraphrasing or summarising what the other person said to confirm understanding — e.g., 'So what you're saying is...' It shows the speaker they have been heard correctly.

💞 Relationships Easy Love & Attachment

What is 'attachment theory' in developmental psychology?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

John Bowlby's theory that early bonds with caregivers shape a child's emotional development and become a template for future relationships. Secure attachment with responsive caregivers leads to healthier adult relationships.

💞 Relationships Medium Psychology

What are the four adult attachment styles identified in research by Hazan and Shaver, and how do they manifest?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Secure (comfortable with intimacy), Anxious-Preoccupied (craves closeness, fears abandonment), Dismissive-Avoidant (values independence, suppresses intimacy), and Fearful-Avoidant (desires intimacy but fears it). Early caregiver relationships shape these styles.

💞 Relationships Medium Conflict & Resolution

What is 'flooding' in the context of couples conflict, and how does Gottman recommend dealing with it?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Flooding is physiological overwhelm during conflict (heart rate spikes above 100 bpm) that impairs rational thinking. Gottman recommends calling a time-out of at least 20 minutes to allow the nervous system to calm before continuing the discussion.

💞 Relationships Medium Love & Attachment

What is 'limerence' and how does it differ from mature love?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Limerence (coined by Dorothy Tennov) is an intense, involuntary state of infatuation with intrusive thoughts and a desperate need for reciprocation. It's driven by neurochemicals (dopamine, norepinephrine) and typically peaks in 18–36 months. Mature love involves deeper commitment, care, and acceptance beyond the obsessive phase.

💞 Relationships Medium Psychology

What is the 'demand-withdrawal' pattern in couple conflict and why is it damaging?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

One partner pushes for discussion while the other withdraws; this creates a cycle of escalating pursuit and avoidance. Research links it to lower relationship satisfaction and higher rates of separation, because core issues never get resolved.

💞 Relationships Medium Communication

What is 'nonviolent communication' (NVC) as developed by Marshall Rosenberg?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

A communication framework with four components: Observations (stating facts without evaluation), Feelings (naming emotions), Needs (identifying underlying needs), and Requests (making clear, doable requests). It aims to foster empathy and resolve conflict without blame.

💞 Relationships Medium Love & Attachment

What does research say about the relationship between self-disclosure and relationship closeness?

◷ Editorial fact review pending

Progressive self-disclosure — sharing increasingly personal information over time — builds intimacy and trust (Social Penetration Theory, Altman & Taylor). Reciprocal disclosure accelerates closeness, and one-sided disclosure without reciprocation can feel intrusive or destabilising.

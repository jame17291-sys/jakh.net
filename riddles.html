41Ten discs of different sizes are stacked largest to smallest on one of three pegs. Move them to another peg, moving one top disc at a time and never placing a larger disc on a smaller one. What is the minimum number of moves?

Answer

1,023. To move n discs, move n−1 aside, move the largest, then move n−1 onto it: T(n)=2T(n−1)+1, with T(1)=1. Thus T(10)=2¹⁰−1.

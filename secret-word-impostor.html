Secret Word Impostor

One friend has no word. Bluff, listen closely, and expose the impostor before they work it out.

Free · No account needed

How to play

Each player privately sees their role; everyone except the impostor gets the word. Give a short clue aloud without saying the word, then pass the phone for a secret vote. Catching the impostor requires more than half the votes and awards no points. Otherwise the impostor earns 2 points. Skipping awards no points.

Keep it kind: anyone can skip a round at any time.

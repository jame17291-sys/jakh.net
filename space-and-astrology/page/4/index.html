61What is the event horizon of a black hole?

Answer

The boundary around a black hole beyond which nothing — including light — can escape its gravitational pull

Answer

The maximum mass (~1.4 solar masses) a white dwarf star can have before collapsing — key to understanding Type Ia supernovae

66What is Hubble's Law?

Answer

Galaxies are receding from us at a speed proportional to their distance — evidence of the expanding universe

67What is Roche limit?

Answer

The minimum distance at which a celestial body, held together by its own gravity, can orbit a larger body without being torn apart by tidal forces

68What is Hawking radiation?

Answer

Theoretical thermal radiation emitted by black holes due to quantum effects near the event horizon, causing them to slowly evaporate

75What is the Lagrange point and why is it useful?

Answer

76What is the information paradox of black holes?

Answer

The conflict between quantum mechanics (information cannot be destroyed) and black hole physics (Hawking radiation appears to destroy information)

77What is inflationary cosmology?

Answer

The theory that the universe underwent exponential expansion in the first fraction of a second after the Big Bang, explaining the flatness and horizon problems

78What is the Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff (TOV) limit?

Answer

The maximum mass of a cold, non-rotating neutron star (about 2–3 solar masses) beyond which it collapses into a black hole

79What is the holographic principle?

Answer

The idea that all information within a volume of space can be described by information on its boundary surface — inspired by black hole thermodynamics

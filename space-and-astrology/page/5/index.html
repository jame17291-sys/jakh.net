81What is the Penrose process?

Answer

82What are Population I, II, and III stars?

Answer

Pop III: first metal-free stars; Pop II: old, metal-poor stars in galactic halos; Pop I: young, metal-rich stars like our Sun

83What is the cosmological constant (Λ)?

Answer

A term in Einstein's field equations representing the energy density of empty space — reintroduced to explain the accelerating expansion of the universe

84What is the Drake Equation?

Answer

A probabilistic formula estimating the number of active, communicating civilisations in the Milky Way using factors like star formation rate, habitable planets, and life emergence probability

85What are gravitational waves and who first detected them?

Answer

Ripples in spacetime caused by accelerating massive objects — first directly detected by LIGO in 2015 from two merging black holes

86What is the s-process vs r-process in stellar nucleosynthesis?

Answer

s-process (slow): neutron capture over long periods in AGB stars; r-process (rapid): fast neutron capture in supernovae or neutron star mergers — creates heavy elements

87What is the Fermi Paradox?

Answer

The contradiction between the high probability of extraterrestrial civilisations and the lack of evidence for, or contact with, such civilisations

88What is spaghettification?

Answer

The tidal stretching of objects into long thin shapes when falling into a black hole due to extreme tidal gravity differences

89What is the concept of stellar evolution and the main sequence?

Answer

Stars spend most of their lives on the main sequence fusing hydrogen in their cores; mass determines lifespan and end state (white dwarf, neutron star, or black hole)

Answer

97What is the Big Rip hypothesis?

Answer

A cosmological model where dark energy causes the universe's expansion to accelerate until it tears apart all matter, from galaxy clusters down to atoms

98What is the Kelvin-Helmholtz mechanism?

Answer

The gravitational contraction of a gas cloud releasing energy as heat and light — the original proposed power source of the Sun before nuclear fusion was understood

99What is baryon acoustic oscillation (BAO)?

Answer

Imprinted patterns in the distribution of matter in the universe from sound waves in the early universe — used as a 'standard ruler' to measure cosmic distances

Answer

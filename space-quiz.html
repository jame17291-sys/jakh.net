Space quiz questions with answers

This space quiz opens with the solar system everyone learns at school, Mars, Saturn's rings, Pluto's demotion and Halley's comet, then follows the human story into orbit: Sputnik, Gagarin, the first Arab astronaut in 1985 and the UAE's Hope probe at Mars. The hard questions reach the Kuiper belt, the parsec and the first photographed black hole, and the last five are for the astronomers: al-Battani's measurement of the year, the Laniakea supercluster, gravitational waves, the Fermi paradox and the signal that was heard once and never again. Every answer is explained in English and Arabic.

29What is the Fermi Paradox?

Answer

The apparent tension between expectations that technological civilizations might be common and the lack of confirmed evidence of them. Their actual probability is not established.
